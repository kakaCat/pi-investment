  - 18个基本面因子 (估值、盈利、成长、质量)
- 因子计算引擎 (支持并行计算)
- 因子缓存机制 (TTL)
- **27个单元测试 (100%通过)**

**代码位置:** `python/factors/`

**性能指标:**
- 计算速度: 0.001秒/因子 (远低于1秒要求)
- 支持批量和并行计算
- 测试覆盖率: 100%

**技术因子列表:**
- 趋势类: MA, EMA, MACD, ADX, SMA, WMA
- 动量类: RSI, KDJ, CCI, ROC, WilliamsR, MOM, STOCH
- 波动类: ATR, BollingerBands, KeltnerChannel, StandardDeviation, HistoricalVolatility, DonchianChannel
- 成交量类: OBV, MFI, VWAP, VolumeRatio, AD, CMF, EMV, ForceIndex

**基本面因子列表:**
- 估值类: PE, PB, PS, PCF, DividendYield, EV_EBITDA
- 盈利能力: ROE, ROA, GrossMargin, NetMargin, ROIC, OperatingMargin
- 成长性: RevenueGrowth, ProfitGrowth, EPSGrowth, PEG, AssetGrowth
- 质量类: DebtToAsset, CurrentRatio, QuickRatio, CashRatio, DebtToEquity, InterestCoverage, AssetTurnover, InventoryTurnover
